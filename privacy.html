Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Controller

The controller responsible for data processing on this website within the meaning of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is:

Represented by managing director Bernd Schürmann. Questions regarding data protection can be directed to the contact details above.

2. Overview

This website serves as a portfolio and information platform for Cashmosphere. It does not operate an online shop, does not use cookies, and — at present — does not employ any analytics, tracking, or advertising tools.

3. Hosting

This website is hosted by Netlify, Inc., 44 Montgomery Street, Suite 300, San Francisco, CA 94104, USA ("Netlify"). When you visit this website, Netlify automatically collects and stores information in server log files, which your browser transmits to us automatically. This includes your IP address, browser type and version, operating system, referrer URL, and the date and time of the request.

This data is processed to ensure the stable and secure operation of the website and is not merged with other data sources. The legal basis is our legitimate interest pursuant to Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. As Netlify is based in the United States, data may be transferred to a third country. Netlify has committed to appropriate safeguards, including Standard Contractual Clauses pursuant to Art. 46 GDPR. Further information is available in Netlify's own privacy policy at netlify.com/privacy.

4. Cookies

This website does not use cookies of any kind.

5. Web fonts

The typeface used on this website is hosted locally on our own server. No connection is established to external font providers, and no data is transmitted to third parties for this purpose.

6. Third-party tools

No analytics, tracking, or advertising tools (such as Google Analytics or the Meta Pixel) are currently in use on this website. Should this change in the future — for example, through the introduction of a newsletter tool — this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly in advance.

7. Contacting us

If you contact us by email or telephone, the information you provide — such as your name, contact details, and the content of your message — will be stored and processed solely for the purpose of handling your inquiry and any related follow-up questions. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, where your inquiry relates to a potential business relationship (such as a business enquiry), or our legitimate interest pursuant to Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR in responding to your inquiry otherwise. Your data will be deleted once it is no longer required for these purposes, unless statutory retention obligations require otherwise.

8. SSL/TLS encryption

This website uses SSL/TLS encryption for all data transmitted between your browser and our server.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • Request rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR)
  • Request erasure of your data (Art. 17 GDPR)
  • Request restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • Receive your data in a portable format (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • Object to the processing of your data (Art. 21 GDPR)

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. The competent authority for Berlin is the Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit).

10. Data security

We apply appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data against loss, misuse, and unauthorized access.

11. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to our website or applicable legal requirements.

A world built around freedom

Cashmosphere defines a lifestyle dedicated to the beauty and versatility of the world's most luxurious fibre. Produced in Mongolia from the finest yarns, Cashmosphere stands for uncompromising quality and craftsmanship. Rooted in excellence, inspired by nature, and defined by authenticity.

Beyond classic elegance, Cashmosphere embraces innovation and playfulness through graphics, prints, intarsia, and open pointelle knits. Prints draw inspiration from Shibori dyeing techniques and bohemian paisley motifs, reinterpreted through a modern lens.

"I wanted to build a cashmere brand with a clear point of view, not another logo, but a material-first idea of quiet luxury," says Bernd Schürmann, founder of Cashmosphere. Schürmann launched the label in Berlin in 2025, rooted in exceptional craftsmanship and driven by innovation. From the very first collection, Creative Director Michael von der Crone has shaped its design language.

With its second collection, Cashmosphere continues to build its place within the international cashmere segment, guided by a commitment to quality and a clear, distinctive design language.
